[Stats::LikeR](https://metacpan.org/pod/Stats::LikeR) provides fast, minimal dependency statistics in Perl.

There's no second language, and writing code in XS means that it's significantly easier on resources than other statistics packages. Results are checked using R and python's own tests to confirm correctness.
