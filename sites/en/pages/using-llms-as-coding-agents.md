## Description

The presentation will address the following topics:

* How do I use agents?
* What differences are there?
* What are my experiences?

Most examples will focus around GLM 5.3 and Perl.

## Speaker Bio:

[Maximilian Maischein](https://www.linkedin.com/in/maximilian-maischein/) aka [Corion](https://corion.net/).

Max is a long-time Perl programmer and contributor. He is co-organizer of the German Perl Workshop and writes Perl code as a long-time hobby. At his dayjob, he combines bank deposits with DLT to create deposit tokens as digital money.
